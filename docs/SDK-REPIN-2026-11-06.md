## Journey chains

The poll-bound tapes were re-recorded on the new journey image (JOURNEY_STATE moved to 0x8008fe30 and frame phase
shifted). The tapes recorded on the real target replay 54/54 on real but drift on the MiSTer core. Truncating the
Start-to-E1M2 tape shows the state is identical on both targets through sample 1180; at sample 1181 (the same stick
bytes) the player steps (+3619, -43834) on real and (+5136, -65767) on the core, a 1.5x step. The SDK flip histogram
after 1181 polls is real {2: 1072, 3: 225, 4: 27, 5: 13, 6: 5, 7: 2} and mister the same except 3: 226, with no
draw-done fallback on either: one frame of about 1,344 flipped one VBlank later on the core under the 30 fps cap,
which changes that tick's catch-up step (the simulation step follows the elapsed VBlanks). Nothing malfunctions; the
open-loop tape is only as portable as the frame phase. `tests/journeys/e1-chain` is the real-target chain and
`tests/journeys/e1-chain-mister` is recorded on the core (`PSOXIDE_HARDWARE=mister` for the bot); each replays 54/54
on its own target. The durable fix is a closed-loop chain that checks positions and state at its cut points.
